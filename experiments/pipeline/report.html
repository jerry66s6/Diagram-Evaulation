Pipeline

  1. Two VLM judges (gpt-6-luna) score each diagram on 13 dimensions: the correctness judge on Layout, Connectivity, Presence, Details, and Legibility, and the Beauty judge on its eight dimensions, with the four tuned prompts for Centering, Space Use, Alignment, and Canvas Fill.
  2. Gradient boosting, trained on the human scores of HS001 to HS200, turns the 13 scores into a good or bad call.
  3. The call is compared with each diagram's true label: positive (good) or negative (deliberately made bad with inserted errors). The human scores of the test diagrams are not used.
