Correctness evaluator vs. human ratings

300 flowcharts from the human study · evaluator model gpt-6-luna · all scores on a 1–10 scale

  • Each flowchart has human ratings for five correctness dimensions: Layout, Connectivity, Presence, Details, Legibility.
  • The evaluator looks at the flowchart and its caption and gives a 1–10 score for each dimension, one dimension at a time.
  • Overall correctness is the mean of the five dimensions, for the humans and for the evaluator alike.
  • Training set: HS001–HS100, where the evaluator's instructions were developed. Validation set: HS101–HS200. Test set: HS201–HS300, used only for testing.
  • 150 of the 300 flowcharts are unmodified originals; the other 150 have one to three inserted errors.

Metrics

Pearson r
Linear correlation between human and evaluator scores, with a 95% confidence interval (Fisher z).
Spearman, Kendall
Rank correlations: do the two put the flowcharts in the same order.
MAE, RMSE
Mean absolute and root-mean-square difference in points; RMSE weighs large differences more.
Within 1 point
Share of flowcharts where the two scores differ by at most 1.
