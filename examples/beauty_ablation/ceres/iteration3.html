Dimension definitions

Aesthetic Craft

Execution quality of shapes, strokes, typography geometry, connectors, arrowheads, edge rendering, and consistency across equivalent elements.

Does not score: Colour choice, ink amount, spacing, and global placement.

Palette

Whether chromatic hues coordinate, remain perceptually distinct, and are used consistently for stable semantic roles.

Does not score: Rendering craft, canvas coverage, spacing, placement, and accessibility contrast.

Ink Balance

The fraction of the total canvas covered by non-background ink, including fills, strokes, text, texture, and decoration.

Does not score: What marks mean, colour harmony, local gaps, and where the foreground group sits.

Density

Local clearance between distinct neighbouring elements relative to their size, including box, label, and connector clearances.

Does not score: Intended connector attachment points, outer whitespace, and global placement.

Balance

Global placement of foreground diagram content, measured through opposing canvas margins and displacement of the visual centroid.

Does not score: Full-canvas backgrounds, local spacing, ink amount, colour, and styling.
