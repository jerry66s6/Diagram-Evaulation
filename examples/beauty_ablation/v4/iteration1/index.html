  • Space Use: Nodes should not feel condensed together while usable canvas space is left empty.
  • Color Consistency: One coherent color theme with no random colors; each role keeps one color.
  • Contrast: Boxes stand out from the background and touching boxes; arrows stand out from the background.

Isolation check

Every intervention lowered only its target dimension under the configured threshold.

Baseline

Centered, well-spread six-node flowchart with a two-hue blue/teal theme on white.

Centering: 99 (runs [100, 99, 99])

Observed: Foreground bounding box is approximately x=195–1006 and y=169–580 on a 1200×750 canvas, giving left/right margins of about 16%/16% and top/bottom margins of about 23%/23%. The visual centroid is essentially at the canvas center.

Both opposing margin pairs are nearly symmetric, with no meaningful displacement of the overall foreground group on either axis.

Space Use: 71 (runs [68, 78, 68])

Observed: After re-centering, the node group spans about 68% of the canvas width and 43% of its height. Horizontal gaps are roughly 85–90% of a node's width, and the vertical gap is about 155% of a node's height, so neighboring nodes are comfortably separated rather than squeezed.

Width use and gaps are generous, but the group occupies only about 43% of the canvas height, leaving substantial usable vertical space. This makes the layout noticeably compact on one axis despite otherwise comfortable spacing.

Color Consistency: 100 (runs [100, 100, 100])

Observed: 2 chromatic hue families: green is used consistently for the Start/End terminal role, and blue is used consistently for all process-step boxes. Arrows are neutral gray and do not count as a theme color. Elements sharing each role use the same hue family.

The diagram uses a coherent two-hue theme with a stable color-to-role mapping: green for terminals and blue for process steps. There are no chromatic role inconsistencies.

Contrast: 96 (runs [96, 96, 96])

Observed: In grayscale, all boxes are distinctly darker than the near-white background, and the dark connectors/arrows also separate clearly. No boxes touch or overlap. The weakest pair is the connectors versus the background, but they remain immediately visible.

Every box and arrow has strong lightness separation from what it sits against. The connectors are somewhat thinner than the box shapes, but their dark value preserves excellent contrast.
