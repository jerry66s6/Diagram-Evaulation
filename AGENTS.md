# Instructions

1. Keep the work simple.
2. Be explicit about each mission's boundary.
3. This is a research project, not a production product. Prioritize clear experiments and simple implementations over production-grade engineering.
4. Do not introduce guardrails, elaborate protocols, or defensive infrastructure unless explicitly requested by the user or essential to the research objective.
5. Do not add hash calculations, checksums, integrity manifests, or similar verification machinery unless the research task specifically requires them.
6. Do not add tests, testing frameworks, or validation pipelines by default. Use only the minimal checks needed to establish research correctness; do not create tests merely to make them pass.
